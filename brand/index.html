FAQ

Good questions.

Does Lunar IPTV Player come with channels?

No. It's a player only. You add a playlist from your own IPTV provider, and Lunar plays it. We don't sell, provide or recommend any content or subscriptions.

Which playlists work?

Xtream Codes accounts (server address, username and password) and M3U links. The TV guide comes from your provider, and the Enhanced TV Guide can add programme names from public XMLTV guides for the regions you choose.

Can I use it on my Android TV box?

Yes. Search for Lunar IPTV Player in the Play Store on your Android TV or Google TV. Every screen works with the remote, and performance mode keeps things smooth on older boxes.

Why won't my playlist play in the browser?

Browsers block http:// addresses on secure websites, and many providers still use them. If yours does, the Android, Android TV and Windows apps play it without any trouble.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no account and no ads.

Where is my data kept?

On your device. Playlists, favorites, watch history and stats stay on the device you use. There's no sign-up and no tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
