# Google Play listing: Lunar IPTV Player

## Short description (80 characters max)

Live TV with a real TV guide, movies and series. Made for your TV remote.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Lunar IPTV Player turns your own IPTV playlist into a clean, fast TV experience on Android phones, tablets and Android TV.

Add your Xtream Codes account or an M3U link, let it sync, and everything is ready: live channels with a proper programme guide, movies and series that remember where you stopped, and a player that feels just as good with a TV remote as with a touch screen.

LIVE TV WITH A REAL GUIDE
• A programme guide timeline with a live "now" line, channel numbers and search
• Watch in the mini player next to the guide, then go full screen with one press
• Change channels from the player, and see what's on now and next
• Enhanced TV Guide fills gaps with real programme names from public XMLTV guides for your region
• Ultra HD channels on devices that can play them
• Reconnects on its own if your provider drops the stream

MOVIES AND SERIES
• Big poster grids with ratings, languages and trailers
• Continue Watching picks up at the exact second you stopped
• Recommended for You and More Like This learn from what you actually finish
• Seasons and episodes in one place, with Resume and Start Over

A CALM, CAPABLE PLAYER
• A loading ring around the play button, with the percentage inside
• Audio and subtitle tracks shown by language name, like English or Spanish, instead of Track 2
• Screen lock so nobody changes the channel by accident. Press Back on the remote or tap the screen to unlock
• Swipe for volume and brightness on phones and tablets

MADE FOR ANDROID TV
• Every screen, button and text field works with the D-pad
• The keyboard only opens when you press OK
• Performance mode keeps menus smooth on older TV boxes with 2 GB of memory

MY STATS
• Total watch time, day streaks and your daily average
• Your top genres, most watched channels and the hours you usually tune in
• Charts update live while you watch

YOUR RULES
• Add as many Xtream Codes or M3U playlists as you like and switch between them
• Lock Live TV categories with a PIN, and hide the ones you never use
• Favorites and Recently Viewed for channels, movies and series
• Landscape everywhere, from phones to the living room TV
• No account, no ads and no tracking. Your playlists and history stay on your device

Also available on Windows from the Microsoft Store, and in your browser at lunar-iptv-app.web.app.

IMPORTANT
Lunar IPTV Player is a media player only. It does not include, sell or provide any channels, playlists, subscriptions or content. You need your own playlist from an IPTV provider. Please only stream content you have the right to watch.
